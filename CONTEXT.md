# Domain Glossary

## WiFi CSI

Channel State Information: per-subcarrier wireless channel measurements that describe how propagation changes between transmitter and receiver. This project studies CSI as the primary sensing signal.

## WiFi RSSI

Received Signal Strength Indicator: aggregate received-power measurement. RSSI is an in-scope comparison signal, not the primary signal for this effort.

## Fall detection

Recognition of a fall event from WiFi-derived measurements. It is distinct from fall-risk prediction and post-fall monitoring.

## Activity recognition

Recognition of everyday activities such as walking, sitting, standing, or lying. It supports fall-detection research when used to define non-fall classes or assess false alarms.

## Paper note

A Markdown analysis of one research publication.

## Topic note

A Markdown synthesis of one research concept, linking to relevant paper and resource notes without duplicating their full analysis.

## Evidence

Information extracted from a paper, dataset, or repository. Evidence is an input to research synthesis, not a POC decision by itself.

## Research synthesis

A reasoned understanding built by comparing evidence from multiple sources. It records agreement, disagreement, limitations, and uncertainty.

## POC decision

A project choice made from research synthesis, such as a hardware setup, dataset, preprocessing approach, model, or evaluation protocol. A POC decision must not depend on one paper alone.

## Open question

An unresolved research or design question recorded in `research/open-questions.md`.
