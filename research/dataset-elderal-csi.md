# ElderAL-CSI

- Type: Dataset
- URL: https://ieee-dataport.org/documents/elderal-csi
- Review status: Initial inventory; verify access and licensing before selection.

## Content

The initial inventory reports standing, walking, falling, sleeping, drinking, and sitting activities.

## CSI and hardware

Reported setup: 2x3 transmit/receive configuration and 512 subcarriers using a ZTE AX3000 router.

## Participants and environment

3 participants in an indoor setup with varied positions.

## Access and license

Not specified in the initial inventory.

## Tooling and preprocessing

Dataset-specific tooling and exact preprocessing: Not reported.

## POC suitability

Potentially useful for a controlled initial activity-recognition and fall-detection experiment. Small participant count limits generalization.
