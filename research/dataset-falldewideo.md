# FallDeWideo

- Type: Dataset
- URL: https://doi.org/10.1145/3615984.3616501
- Review status: Initial inventory; verify modalities, access, and license.

## Content

The initial inventory reports WiFi CSI data paired with video, covering falls and daily activities.

## CSI and hardware

Intel 5300 NIC and mixed devices were reported.

## Participants and environment

Varied volunteers and multiple settings were reported; exact details: Not reported.

## Access and license

Not specified in the initial inventory.

## Scope note

The dataset combines WiFi CSI with video. Other modalities are outside this effort's current scope, so this resource should not drive the initial single-modality POC unless the scope changes.

## POC suitability

Potentially useful for understanding labeling or supervision, but not currently a direct candidate.
