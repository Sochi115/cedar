# CSI-Bench

- Type: Dataset and benchmark
- URL: https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2505.21866
- Review status: Initial inventory; preprint and access details require verification.

## Content

The initial inventory reports fall detection, breathing monitoring, localization, and other tasks.

## CSI and hardware

Varied device configurations were reported, including 1x1 and 2x2 MIMO configurations across commercial WiFi edge devices.

## Participants and environment

35 users across 26 environments were reported.

## Access and license

Not specified in the initial inventory.

## Tooling and preprocessing

A pipeline for efficient data collection was reported. Exact data format and preprocessing tools: Not reported.

## POC suitability

Potentially valuable for testing generalization across users and environments. Verify direct fall labels and access before relying on it.
