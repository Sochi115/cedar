# WmFall: WiFi-Based Multistage Fall Detection

- Year: 2018
- URL: https://doi.org/10.1177/1550147718805718
- Review status: Initial pass; verify against full paper before relying on quantitative claims.

## Research question

Whether a multistage WiFi CSI system can distinguish falls from similar daily activities.

## Hardware and CSI setup

WiFi devices were used to analyze CSI. Device, antenna, and CSI extraction details: Not reported in the initial pass.

## Participants and environment

Laboratory testing was reported. Participant count and environment details: Not reported.

## Dataset

The initial report describes falls and sitting as evaluated activities.

## Preprocessing

Raw CSI segmentation and feature extraction were reported. Exact parameters: Not reported.

## Features and model

A multistage fall-detection approach was used. Detailed model stages: Not reported.

## Evaluation protocol

Evaluation covered common scenarios. Split design: Not reported.

## Results

Reported accuracy: 89%. Reported false-alarm rate: 8%.

## Limitations

False alarms in complex environments were reported.

## Relevance to this project

Useful for studying multistage detection and false-alarm trade-offs.
