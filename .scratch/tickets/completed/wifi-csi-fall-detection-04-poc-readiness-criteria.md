## Question

What criteria must research synthesis satisfy before the project makes defensible POC decisions about hardware, dataset, and evaluation protocol?

Define the minimum synthesis quality needed for a decision. The decision must compare multiple sources, identify agreement and disagreement, record limitations and uncertainty, and explain why the selected approach fits the project. Individual papers, datasets, and repositories provide evidence; they do not determine a POC decision alone.

## Answer

Research readiness has no fixed metric. The project owner decides when the research synthesis is ready. The synthesis must use all available relevant information, not only paper notes. It should compare sources, record agreement and disagreement, show limitations and uncertainty, and explain possible choices without treating one source as decisive.

POC decisions will be recorded later in `research/poc-decisions.md`. That file is not created during the research phase.
