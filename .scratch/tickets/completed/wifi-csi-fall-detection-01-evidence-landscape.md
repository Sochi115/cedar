## Question

What does existing peer-reviewed research demonstrate about WiFi CSI-based fall detection?

Investigate primary research papers and extract recurring hardware setups, CSI collection methods, environments, participant and activity coverage, preprocessing, features, models, evaluation protocols, results, limitations, and reproducibility. Identify candidate topic groupings that can later become topic notes. Prefer original papers and canonical publication pages. Mark uncertainty and missing methodology details.

## Answer

Initial pass documented five candidate papers and recurring concerns: controlled indoor environments, limited participant diversity, environmental sensitivity, false alarms, and incomplete reporting of split design and hardware details. Reported headline metrics are not yet comparable and require full-paper verification.
