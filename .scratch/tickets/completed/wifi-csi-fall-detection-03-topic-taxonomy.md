## Question

What initial set of topic notes gives humans a clear path through the evidence landscape without duplicating paper notes?

Define topic names, boundaries, relationships, and paper-linking rules using the completed evidence and resource inventories. Include WiFi RSSI only where it clarifies CSI-related choices.

## Answer

Adopt seven flat topic notes under `research/`:

- `topic-csi-vs-rssi.md`
- `topic-hardware-and-csi-extraction.md`
- `topic-preprocessing-and-representation.md`
- `topic-features-and-models.md`
- `topic-datasets-and-activities.md`
- `topic-evaluation-and-generalization.md`
- `topic-reproducibility.md`

Topic notes provide short synthesis, comparison tables, source links, limitations, and open-question links. Paper notes link to multiple relevant topics; topic notes link back to papers and resources without duplicating full paper analysis. RSSI has its own comparison topic. Fall-risk prediction and non-WiFi modalities remain outside scope.
